One workforce. Multiple ways to verify attendance.

Choose the supported workflow that fits the operation.

Shiftly supports different attendance methods depending on the business, site setup and configured devices.

01 / QR

Scan, identify and capture.

An employee QR code can be scanned through the Shiftly clocking screen. The employee is added to the clocking queue and the authorised user submits the Clock IN or Clock OUT event.

  • Digital employee identity
  • Supervisor-supported scanning workflow
  • Attendance captured online

02 / LOCATION

GPS captured. Geofence enforced.

Location supplies the device position. Geofencing goes further by comparing that position with the permitted site area and its configured radius before the event is submitted.

  • High-accuracy location requested
  • Approved site radius configured
  • Outside-site attempts blocked

03 / FACE

Face-ready where configured.

Shiftly supports face enrolment and a face-scanning clocking mode where applicable. The interface looks for an enrolled employee match before adding the employee to the attendance flow.

  • Employee face enrolment
  • Camera-based scan mode
  • Available where applicable
