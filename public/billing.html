Recurring monthly drafts

Take the repetition out of recurring invoices.

Configure a reusable monthly template with the client, line items, invoice day and payment terms. Shiftly prepares a draft invoice for review when the scheduled date arrives.

  • Active or paused templates
  • Start date and optional end date
  • Invoice day and due-after terms
  • Next draft and recent run visibility

Shiftly prepares drafts. It does not automatically charge the customer.

Details worth reusing

Set up the records behind every document.

Keep client contacts and billable products or services ready for the next quote, then maintain the company details that belong on your documents.

01

Client records

Company name, contact person, email, phone, VAT number, address and active status.

02

Billable items

Item code, description, unit, price and VAT treatment for reusable products or services—not inventory.

Document settings

Registration and VAT details, contact information, banking details, payment terms and default invoice notes.
